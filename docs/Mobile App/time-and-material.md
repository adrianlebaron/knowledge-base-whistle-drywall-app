This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough for creating a new Time and Material entry from scratch, including entering job details, adding labor and materials, and completing the setup within the app.

---

## Step 1: Open the Time & Material Module
From the left navigation menu in the mobile app, tap the **hamburger menu**.
![1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e0467bb6-11f4-46d2-a5dd-c420e48035d2)

## Step 2: Once open select the Time and Material module.
![2](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c880cb24-f318-48c2-bd52-2b18e5b7ea68)

## Step 3: Once there be shown screen will display a list of existing entries and the option to create a new one.
![3](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/869fda68-36a1-4a63-a8b3-653496eaad39)

## Step 4: Here is the interface to create a new entry, colapse the drop the dropdown menu by activate it.
![4](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/035884f2-a0e3-4837-8501-5e550112cd43)

## Step 5: Select the job associated with the work being performed on. This ensures the Time and Material entry is properly linked to the correct project.
