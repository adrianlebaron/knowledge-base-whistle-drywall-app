This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough for creating a new Time and Material entry from scratch, including entering job details, adding labor and materials, and completing the setup within the app.

---

## Step 1: Open the Time & Material Module

From the left navigation menu in the mobile app, tap the **hamburger menu**.
![1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8c94765d-1a92-4d49-93f3-ef48a584f922)

## Step 2:

Once open select the Time and Material module.
![2](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f51d1f79-6b20-4d90-822c-285a8838e313)

## Step 3:

Once there be shown screen will display a list of existing entries and the option to create a new one.
![3](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/db4990f8-336e-4c1a-9d4b-7c77cc70cc96)

## Step 4:

Here is the interface to create a new entry, colapse the drop the dropdown menu by activate it.

![4](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cc0d4a69-7276-4ddc-83bf-774b3e778854)

Select the job associated with the work being performed on. This ensures the Time and Material entry is properly linked to the correct project.
