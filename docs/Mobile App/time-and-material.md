This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough for creating a new Time and Material entry from scratch, including entering job details, adding labor and materials, and completing the setup within the app.

---

## To star look for the side navigation menu.
From the top left corner, tap the **hamburger menu**.
![1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e0467bb6-11f4-46d2-a5dd-c420e48035d2)

## Step 1: Open the Time & Material Module
Once open select the Time and Material module.
![2](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c880cb24-f318-48c2-bd52-2b18e5b7ea68)

## Step 3: T&M module overview.
Once there be shown screen will display a list of existing entries and the option to create a new one.
![3](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/869fda68-36a1-4a63-a8b3-653496eaad39)

## Step 4: Access dropdown job list.
Here is the interface to create a new entry, colapse the drop the dropdown menu by activate it.
![4](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/035884f2-a0e3-4837-8501-5e550112cd43)

## Step 5: Job selction. 
Select the job associated with the work being performed on. This ensures the Time and Material entry is properly linked to the correct project.
![5](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0785cffc-24a5-4958-9833-f8bf1394bb1a)

## Step 6: Describe the Work Performed.
Provide a brief but clear description of the specific work completed.
This description helps project managers and office staff quickly understand the purpose of the entry.
![6](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/252afa92-c7be-462b-9a7b-3ae76b1ae36d)

## Step 7: Define the General Scope of Work.
Explain the overall task or activity performed on site.
This field should clearly describe what work was done and how it was performed.
![7](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd5069b0-c45f-4f41-85c1-8cdf02930aee)

## Step 8: Specify the Reason for the Work.
Indicate why the work was required.
Providing a reason helps justify the Time and Material work for approvals and billing.
![8](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e98a1946-8f14-421c-bf6b-44c37c64c841)

## Step 9: Enter the Location of the Work.
Specify where on the job site the work took place.
This is especially useful for large projects with multiple levels or zones.
![9](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/954e560b-49bc-490a-83dc-57fcdbd7091a)

## Step 10: Select the Date of Work.
Choose the date when the work was performed.
Always ensure the date matches the actual on-site work.
The arrows let you navigate thru months, and just select the date desire then jusst hit OK to save changes. 
![11](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d163e810-96a7-4139-8b45-b2b318c5e28d)

## Step 11: Complete Subcontractor and Authorization Details.
If the work was performed on behalf of another subcontractor, complete the fields related to subcontractor responsibility and authorization.
![12](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3d8cd479-ed87-4030-8a2c-764a66782a75)
